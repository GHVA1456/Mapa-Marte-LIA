{{PROFILE}}

Why this route

Three Forks is the flat spot at the base of the delta where Perseverance built its sample depot. The walk follows the rover's own wheel tracks up to the delta front, layered rock laid down when a river fed a lake here. Ground that carried a one-tonne rover is the best-verified path available, and its tilt record shows where the slope steepens.

Elevations in the profile were measured by the rover. The 3D terrain uses a 200 m orbital model, so the two differ by a few tens of metres.

Resources for the stay

    Per astronaut per day: 0.84 kg oxygen and about 1 gallon (3.8 L) of water (NASA). MOXIE's best rate on Mars was 12 g of oxygen per hour. Food is left out because we have no verified NASA figure for it yet.

    Atmosphere and pressure

    About 95% carbon dioxide at roughly 6 mbar, under 1% of Earth's pressure. Nothing to breathe, and water boils near body temperature, so a suit breach is an immediate emergency.

    Temperature and dust

    NASA gives a median of −65 °C with a range from about 20 °C down to −153 °C. Dust can stay aloft for months after a storm and gets into seals, filters and solar panels.

    Reading the thermal layers

    In the THEMIS night image, bright ground stayed warm after sunset: rock or cemented soil. Dark ground cooled fast: loose dust or sand, which is harder to walk on.

    Radiation

    Mars has no global magnetic field and a thin atmosphere. Curiosity's RAD measured an average galactic cosmic ray dose equivalent of 0.67 mSv per day at Gale Crater (August 2012 to June 2013), about 210 µGy per day absorbed. Solar particle events add to this without warning, so every EVA needs a shelter plan.

    Gravity

    3.71 m/s², 38% of Earth's. Loads feel lighter but keep their inertia, and long missions bring bone loss, muscle atrophy and vision changes (SANS).

    Distance

    A radio signal takes 4 to 24 minutes each way. The crew must be able to decide alone.

    Water

    MRO's SHARAD radar has mapped buried ice-rich deposits, including one in Utopia Planitia. Mining it is an option to prove, not a capability that exists.

    Oxygen

    MOXIE, on Perseverance, made 122 g of oxygen from Martian CO₂ across 16 runs. A crewed mission needs a plant hundreds of times larger.

    Habitat and life support

    Shelter must stop radiation and dust and hold pressure. The ISS now recovers about 98% of its water, a benchmark for a Mars habitat, not a guarantee.

    Crew autonomy

    NASA's CHAPEA missions put four people in a 1,700 ft² habitat for a year with limited supplies and delayed communications to study how crews cope.

    What this map does not claim: the route is a planning sketch on rover-driven ground, not an approved traverse. Tilt is the rover's attitude at each stop. Red zones are slopes over 20° computed from the 20 m USGS model. The CRISM layers are NASA's published browse images, shown without interpretation.
